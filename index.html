The World of Gorillas

Majestic, Intelligent, and Endangered

About These Giants

Gorillas are ground-dwelling, predominantly herbivorous apes that inhabit the tropical forests of equatorial Africa. They share about 98% of their DNA with humans!

Physical Characteristics

Height: 4 - 6 feet
Weight: 300 - 500 lbs
Lifespan: 35 - 40 years
